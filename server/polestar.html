Connect your Polestar

Sign in with the email and password you use for the Polestar app. Hearth reads the car's mileage once a day, so the cost per mile uses miles you actually drove.

Your password goes straight to Polestar's sign-in and isn't stored. Hearth keeps only the sign-in token Polestar gives back, as the Polestar app does. It only reads the mileage and battery; it can't unlock, start or change anything.
