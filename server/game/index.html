Click a Sherman, or one of your riflemen to take the whole squad; shift-drag to box several. Click the ground to move, a German to target him. Drag the map or use the arrow keys. Riflemen take a point by standing on it. Hedgerows block sight; tanks need a gap (or can crash through, slowly), and infantry can climb the banks. With the TV remote: arrows move the cursor, OK gives orders, Back pauses.
Hedgerows near Carentan Normandy, June 1944. Two Shermans and 1st Squad have to take a farm and a crossroads in the bocage, then hold both when the Germans counter-attack. The Germans have Panzerfausts and an MG42, and the hedges are thick.
Sound on is best. On the TV, use left and right, then OK.
