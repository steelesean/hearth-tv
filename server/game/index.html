How to play
SelectClick a Sherman or a rifleman (the whole squad), or a card at the bottom; shift-drag a box round several
MoveClick the ground. Click a German to make him the target
OrdersRight-click: the command wheel for the selected units, at that spot. Shift-click: then move here, a further leg after the current move
BeforeAfter Start, plan the attack: the intelligence is drawn on the map; click a unit, then a spot inside the dashed line, to place it; choose your support; then begin
PlanP pauses, and you can still give every order: they go out when you carry on. The game pauses itself when enemy armour appears, a squad breaks or an attack comes (switch this off on the start screen)
KeysF smoke · T fire at a spot · H hold HE · C crash through hedges · G assault · X ambush · U ride a tank · B blow a gap · R back down the lane · Space halt · V artillery · N smoke shells
GameP pause · + double speed · M sound · L radio log · I intelligence · ? this help · arrows scroll
TV remoteArrows move the cursor, OK selects or opens the wheel, Back clears or pauses
ArmourA StuG is thick in front and thin at the sides and back. Your bazooka team fires at any within 110 m; put them in ambush (X) on its flank and they wait until it’s within 60 m
PointsYour riflemen take a point by standing on it with no Germans close
