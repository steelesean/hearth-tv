Tado

Connect Tado to split gas into heating by room, and hot water and cooking.

Your Tado account allows about 1,000 requests a day, shared with the TV's bedtime check. This uses about five a day, and always leaves at least 300 spare.
Ohme

Connect the Ohme charger to use its own record of each charge: exact kWh, miles added and cost per mile.

Scan with your phone and sign in with your Ohme app details. The password goes to Ohme only and isn't stored. Read-only: it never changes your charging.
